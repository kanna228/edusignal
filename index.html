Group data (school type, language, work) is not an input to the indicator: it is held separately by the data protection officer and used only for this check.

    Rule: a gap of more than 10 percentage points between groups is marked gap and must be reviewed by people before the next trimester.

    MACHINE Finding 1: rural students get more false alarms

    34% of flags for rural-school students were false alarms, against 18% for urban.

    HUMAN Likely cause and fix

    The "no LMS login" signal penalises students with unstable home internet who download materials on campus. Proposed: lower the weight of logins, add in-class attendance.

    MACHINE Finding 2: working students accept help less often

    44% of contacted working students accepted help, against 63% of others.

    HUMAN Likely cause and fix

    Tutoring is only offered in daytime slots. Proposed: evening and online tutoring, and offer the flexible deadline first.

    HUMAN Who decides the changes

    Advisors, a student representative and the data protection officer review this report every trimester. Every change to the indicator is logged.
